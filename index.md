### AquaLink — Entrepreneurial Work-Integrated Learning

**Documented team role: Backend Developer.**

AquaLink is a proposed community water supply and management platform for **Muraleni, Limpopo**. The project considered household connections, borehole suppliers, subscriptions, water usage and operational oversight. The group explored a network of physical water infrastructure supported by a digital system.

My charter-assigned workstream involved **REST APIs, relational database design and business logic**. The five Agile documents show the team's planning and source evidence; any specific claim of implemented code or completed tests requires my own evidence.
