## AquaLink — Entrepreneurial WIL

**Role documented in the shared team charter:** Backend Developer.

AquaLink is a student team project concerning reliable water access for households in Muraleni, Limpopo. The proposed solution connects households, borehole suppliers and administrators through a digital management platform, with a planned physical water-distribution network.

My documented role covers **REST API design, relational data modelling and business logic**. Specific implementations, test outcomes and dates must be confirmed against my own development records before being described as completed work.
