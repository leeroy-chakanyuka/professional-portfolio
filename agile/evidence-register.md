## What can and cannot be asserted

The shared evidence supports **the planned team roles**, the existence of Agile artefacts, the reported early sprint dates and the project concept. It does **not**, by itself, establish:

- Exactly which AquaLink Trello cards Leeroy completed and on which dates.
- Which API endpoints, database changes or automated tests Leeroy personally implemented.
- Leeroy's own daily stand-ups, attendance, blockers or retrospective decisions.
- Whether every planned AquaLink feature is deployed and functioning.

The separate Project 3 document involving Tony Baloyi as a frontend lead concerns another student project; its responsibility table must **not** be substituted for the AquaLink charter.

## Attribution and individual reflection

The portfolio uses original source links for the teammates' images and documents. The individual reflection paragraphs are working drafts grounded in the team history and assigned backend role. Before assessment submission, they should be checked against Leeroy's actual experiences and amended wherever necessary. The personal logbook cannot be fabricated from someone else's dated record.
